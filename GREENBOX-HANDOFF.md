- Never append `// comment` in the middle of a line with sed / replace: it comments out the rest of the line.
- `const` names declared twice in one function break the build (rolldown "already declared").

  - `window.__gb3d` = the create-page engine (`.easy_rig`, `.load_model_step.model_meshes()`, `.skeleton_helper`).
  - `window.__vehicle` = VehicleApp.
  - `window.__gbHp` = hpbar.
  - `window.__playtest` = PlaytestApp.
- Read bone world positions after `root.updateMatrixWorld(true)` or with `getWorldPosition`.

## 3. Human rig: how it works (create.html)

- `rig/EasyRig.ts`: the joint editor panel and the auto-fit.
  - `fit_to_model()` → `scan_fit()` (body), then `fit_fingers_to_hand()` and `detect_fingers()` (hands), then `auto_weight_fixes()`.
  - Panel sections fold away.
  - Finger joints are NOT locked by default (`finger_lock = false`): click or box-select and drag.
- `rig/BodyScan.ts` `scan_humanoid()` (slices the surface along Y):
  - Finds the crotch (legs split), the torso width, and the neck (the narrowest column with something wider above).
  - **Arms**: points outside the torso, trimmed to the "tube" around the arm (grown from the farthest points), so wide shorts or skirts do not count. Then a principal axis.
  - **Wrist** = the thinnest arm section just before the palm widens (big chibi hands).
  - Thin arms: a side needs at least max(60, 0.25 % of the points).
- `rig/FingerFinder.ts`:
  - `find_fingers()`: fingers that stick out (separate tips).
  - `palm_fit()`: "measured hand", used when the fingers are glued together or `fingers_ok()` rejects the scan.
    - Hand axis, width and normal.
    - **Finger lanes** = bumps of the "how far the hand reaches" profile across the hand (`finger_lanes`), only where the reach is high (the thumb reaches less).
    - Knuckles at 45 % of the hand; joints at 0 / 45 / 75 / 97 % of the finger.
    - Thumb from beyond the index lane.
  - `fingers_ok()` rejects scans that are reversed, too short, or clumped (tips closer than 0.1 × hand width).
  - `hand_reach()`: region radius.
- Weights: `skin/CageWeights.ts` (anatomy cage, its view hidden by default), `skin/WeightPost.ts`, finger weights (`refine_finger_weights`).
- Clone rig for creatures: `rig/CloneFit.ts` (body frame, landmarks, TPS warp), `rig/CustomRig.ts`, `rig/CloneRigWizard.ts`.
  - The wizard sits on the left, can fold, and shows only the current step's body.
  - A vehicle rig dropped into the wizard goes to the vehicle page.

## 4. Vehicles (vehicle.html, `vehicle/`)

- `VehicleApp.ts`:
  - Model → `set_model` (bake, drop scenery into `dropped`, align, centre) → `scan_wheels` → `doors_from_names` (parts by name, see `role_of` in `Samples.ts`).
  - Then `build_rig` → `make_clips` (+ `clone_clips`).
  - Undo works through `History<VState>`.
- **Quick cut** (`q_*` methods):
  - Select with a click on a piece, ⬚ 4 points, or ◯ circle.
  - A menu asks "this piece is a…": 1 wheel, 2 door, 3 hood, 4 trunk, 5 gull-wing, 6 suicide door, 7 turret, 8 barrel, more.
  - `q_detach` cuts it to its own mesh with holes filled.
  - G drags it out, Delete removes it, `[` `]` resize the wheel rings, O hides them.
- 🧹 objects list: `refresh_objects()` (✕ remove, ◎ select, ↺ bring back what the auto-drop removed).
- `Samples.ts`: 14 sample vehicles built from named meshes (`make_sample`), and `role_of(name)` (wheel / rim / door / hood / trunk / turret / barrel / track / boom / arm / bucket / hook / cable / rotor / propeller / forks / antenna…).
- `VehicleClone.ts`: **clone a vehicle rig**.
  - Source (rigged file or sample) and target are put in the same frame (length along Z, front at +Z from wheel names) and squeezed into a unit box.
  - Each target vertex takes the part of the nearest source vertex.
  - The target's own separate wheels are kept.
  - One source node driving both rims of an axle is split left / right.
  - Source animations are replayed as deltas from rest, in the target frame (`retarget_clips`, clips named `SRC_…`).
  - Body translation goes to the root.
  - Scenery around the source is dropped (only what is near the wheels is kept).
- Clone box UI (top of the vehicle page):
  - ① source rig (sample or a rigged file) → ② new vehicle (no bones) → ③ applied automatically.
  - A bone-less, animation-less file put in ① is used as ② instead (the user found the flow "reversed" before this).
- `Mechanics.ts`: part kinds, pivots and clips. `Tracks.ts`: caterpillar belts.
